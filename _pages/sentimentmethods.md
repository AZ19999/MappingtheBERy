# The Tool

Early in the project, I was introduced to a variety of tools related to sentiment and textual analysis. Out of all of them, I was given a pretty comprehensive demo of ProQuest TDM, which I decided to use for this project. ProQuest TDM is a text and data mining platform that enables researchers to analyze vast amounts of content from ProQuest’s extensive database. Their database includes a variety of sources including newspapers, magazines, thesis dissertations, and many more. I mainly focused on the visualization dashboard which included three preset tools that could be automatically run on collections of up to 10,000 documents. These three tools were Topic modeling, Geographic referencing, and most importantly, Sentiment analysis.

# The Method

Firstly, to get a general timeline of sentiment analysis. I set my search to just "Boston Elevated Railway" to get a general sense of how much refining I would need to do. Starting general was key so that I would have some base to compare with whenever I had to look into more specific topics.
